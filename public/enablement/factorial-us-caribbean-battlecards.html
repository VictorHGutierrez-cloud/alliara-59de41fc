US & Caribbean · SMB · Always English

Battle cards: Factorial vs HiBob & Personio

Your market hears culture platforms and EU HR brands every week. You win by selling operations reality: time, shifts, spend, payroll-ready data, and one employee lifecycle — at SMB speed.

Positioning line (memorize): “HiBob wins hearts. Personio wins European HR admin. Factorial wins the weekly operating system — time, people, and money on one record — so US & Caribbean SMBs stop living in spreadsheets.”

1. Market POV · US & Caribbean SMB

What buyers feel
  • English-first teams across offices / islands
  • Hospitality, retail, services, clinics, agencies
  • Accountant / local payroll provider stays in the loop
  • Fear of overpaying for “pretty culture software”
  • Need mobile clock-in, leave, expenses that managers actually use
How you land

Default land package

Core + Time Off + Time Tracking

+ Shifts if rotating staff · + Expenses if receipt chaos · + Recruitment if hiring spike

“Let’s fix the weekly chaos first. Culture modules can come once the operating rhythm is clean.”

2. vs HiBob (Bob)

HiBob = engagement / culture-first, modern UX, often premium. Strong when the buyer wants “people experience.” Vulnerable when the buyer needs ops depth + value.

Where HiBob is strong
  • Employee experience / culture vibe
  • Polished UI and brand story
  • Performance / compensation narratives for scaling tech-ish teams
  • Global mid-market positioning
Where you attack
  • Ops stack: time tracking depth, shifts, spaces, project hours
  • Money path: expenses OCR, cards, procurement → payroll sync
  • IT + offboarding access tied to HR changes
  • SMB value / modular land without buying a culture palace first
  • Caribbean / multi-site workforce ops (schedules, mobile, leave)

Kill soundbites (HiBob)

“Bob is beautiful for culture. The question for you is month-end: shifts, missing punches, expenses, and payroll inputs. Do you want a social feed — or an operating system that feeds your accountant clean data?” “If engagement is the only pain, Bob is a fair shortlist. If managers still chase timesheets and leave on WhatsApp, you’re buying the wrong layer first.” “Let’s price the problem you feel every Friday — not the brochure. Start Core + Time, then add performance when the fire is out.”
HiBob leansCulture, EX, mid-market polish
Factorial leansTime/ops, spend, IT-HR loop, SMB land speed
Watch-outDon’t trash culture — re-sequence: ops first, EX later

3. vs Personio

Personio = European HRIS powerhouse (esp. DACH mental model): compliance, recruiting, structured HR ops. In US & Caribbean deals, it often feels “great for Europe, heavier / less local” unless the buyer is EU-centric.

Where Personio is strong
  • Structured HR processes & recruiting reputation
  • European compliance narrative / GDPR heritage
  • Brand trust with EU HQs expanding abroad
  • Broad HR lifecycle coverage
Where you attack (US & Caribbean)
  • Fit to market: English-first US/Caribbean ops story, not EU payroll theater
  • All-in-one ops + finance adjacency: expenses, projects, shifts, spaces, IT
  • Faster SMB commercial clarity: land small, expand on same employee record
  • Partner payroll model: work with existing US / island payroll providers instead of forcing EU-native assumptions

Kill soundbites (Personio)

“Personio is excellent if your center of gravity is European HR compliance. Your team runs US & Caribbean operations — English-first, local payroll partners, multi-site schedules. Factorial is built as the operating layer for that reality.” “If you pick Personio because ‘everyone in Europe uses it,’ test one scenario: a Friday night shift change + expense + leave balance hitting payroll variables. That’s where we earn the deal.” “We’re not asking you to rip out your accountant. We’re asking to stop sending them messy inputs.”

Integrity note: never invent false “Personio doesn’t do X.” Attack fit, gravity, and breadth of ops/finance/IT on one spine — then prove in demo.

6. 20-minute competitive demo path

When competing with HiBob
  1. Acknowledge culture need (30 sec)
  2. Implication: month-end ops cost
  3. People Path: Clock in → PTO → Lock payroll
  4. Optional: Expenses OCR
  5. Close: land Time/Core this month; EX later
When competing with Personio
  1. Ask where company gravity is (US/Caribbean vs EU HQ)
  2. People Path: Onboarding → Shifts/Time → Expenses → Payroll lock
  3. Show partner-payroll collaboration narrative
  4. Close: operating fit test with local managers (English)
