1. Thesis — Features era is over

HR/WFM platforms commoditized. GenAI equalized information. Catalog demos no longer win.

Differentiate with deep diagnosis, risk mitigation, and alignment with an often-invisible buying committee. Sell resilience — not a module warehouse.

Seller takeawayIf your first three moves are capabilities, status quo is already winning.

2. Decision fatigue

More features can freeze the buyer. Holistic demos trigger implementation anxiety.

Kill pattern

Module dump

Show everything Factorial can do on call one. Buyer concludes transition > current pain.

Win pattern

Curation

One urgent fracture. Land Core + Time Off + Time Tracking. Expand Expenses/ATS/Shifts only if that is the fire.

Seller takeawayLand narrow. Expand on the same employee record later.

3. Loss aversion — risk before ROI

Losses feel ~2× as painful as equivalent gains feel good. Fear of failed implementation beats abstract efficiency promises.

~59%Prefer risk-mitigation frame
~24%Prefer features frame
~17%Prefer ROI frame

Training benchmark from curriculum research on simulated committees — use as dogma for pitch order.

Pattern: Risk today → Cost of waiting → Proof Factorial removes that risk → Then expansion roadmap.
Seller takeawayMake staying look riskier than switching — then prove safety with product.

5. Dark Funnel + GenAI

Most of the journey is anonymous. Buyers shortlist before talking to you. Research:seller time often ~5:1.

Gartner tension

~67% want a rep-free journey — yet ~69% still ask reps to validate AI insights. AI cannot give psychological safety.

Your opening

Assume an AI-built comparison already exists. Correct framing; don’t restart HRIS 101.

Seller takeaway“What did ChatGPT/G2 tell you about us vs HiBob/Personio — and what felt incomplete?”

6. Seller role — context oracle

  1. Verify AI premises (and hallucinations).
  2. Reduce friction and fear.
  3. Lend trust capital through cognitive empathy — understand their constraints, don’t mirror their anxiety.
Seller takeawayYou are not a catalog. You make an algorithmically prepared decision feel safe.

7. Empathy & trust

Trust is emotional first, rational second. Cognitive empathy beats “feeling their stress.”

Affective empathy

Feeling what they feel

HR vents about burnout — you nod along. Helps rapport, but doesn’t close CFO deals.

Cognitive empathy

Mapping their constraints

CFO fears budget overrun — you map how Factorial reduces payroll correction risk. This predicts buyer satisfaction.

Four practice drills

  • Observer drill — watch for guardedness before pitching
  • Strategic silence — one strong question, then wait for fear to surface
  • Shelf the expert — they know their business; you diagnose
  • Objections as fear — validate loss aversion before reframing
“Before we talk product — I want to understand what’s actually at stake for you this quarter. Not the feature list, but what keeps you up at night if this implementation goes wrong. Can we start there?”
Seller takeawayMap their constraint; don’t mirror their anxiety.

8. Factorial positioning

Central nervous system — surgical pain → cure. Factorial One = governance-safe cognitive capacity, not chatbot cosplay.

Pain → sell this (not that)
  • Retention: visibility across goals/satisfaction/burnout — not “performance module”
  • Compliance risk: spreadsheet liability vs controlled time/payroll inputs
  • Month-end chaos: time + leave → payroll-ready variables
  • AI leakage fear: Factorial One inside the corporate wall
Seller takeawayOne fracture per first meeting. The other ~90% is expansion insurance.

11. WFM psychology

Ops wants predictability. Workers want fairness and agency. Mobile-first self-service is non-negotiable.

  • Forecasting · real-time adherence · compliance matrices
  • Shift swap / availability · explainable fairness
  • Hospitality & Caribbean tourism: coverage + fairness beats culture feeds
Seller takeawayBalance control and dignity — or the frontline rejects the tool.

13. Field kits

Pre-call checklist (print/use)

  • Primary pain hypothesis
  • Persona (owner / HR / finance / ops)
  • Land bundle chosen
  • Only 3 People Path beats planned
  • Dated ask prepared
  • AI shortlist assumption (competitors)

Empathy prompts

  • “If this project fails publicly, who takes the hit — and what does that look like?”
  • “What would make you comfortable enough to champion this internally?”

Role-play (5 min): Partner plays skeptical CFO. 60 seconds — no product, only map constraint + validate one fear.

Risk-first opener

“Before any product tour: most teams I meet already researched tools with AI. I’m not here to re-list modules. I want 10 minutes on the risk you’re carrying this quarter — timesheets, leave chaos, or spend hitting payroll dirty — and whether staying as-is is actually safer than changing.”

Demo map

  • Onboarding pain: Start onboarding
  • Time/leave: Clock in → Request PTO
  • Money: Scan receipt → Approve → Lock payroll
  • vs HiBob: Time → payroll lock first
  • vs Personio: Onboarding → time/shifts → expenses → payroll
Practice on People Path MEDDPICC / SPIN
Seller takeawayOne principle per live call until it becomes muscle memory.

  3. Dark Funnel + AI validator role — 15′
  4. Cognitive empathy rewrite — constraint-mapping opener — 10′
